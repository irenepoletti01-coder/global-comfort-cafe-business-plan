# Global Comfort Café: Strategic Feasibility Study & 5-Year Financial Model ☕📈

## 📌 Executive Summary
A comprehensive corporate venture business plan and 5-year financial feasibility analysis developed during an academic exchange in Lille, France. The project assesses the commercial viability of an experiential, multicultural hospitality concept featuring rotating monthly international culinary themes.

As **Chief Executive Officer (CEO)**, I spearheaded the strategic design, operational alignment, and enterprise risk evaluation, working in synergy with the finance team to oversee the integrated 5-year financial modeling (Income Statement, Cash Flow, Balance Sheet, and Capital Structure).

---

## 💼 Core Financial Modeling & Projections (5-Year Horizon)

### 1. Capital Structure & Initial Funding
* **Total Initial Investment:** €50,000 required to launch operations and fund early working capital.
  * *Capital Expenditures (CapEx):* €30,000 allocated for specialized commercial brewing assets, modular interior fittings, and equipment.
  * *Initial Inventory & Marketing:* €5,000 for initial beverage/pastry stock and €5,000 for brand setup and local customer acquisition.
  * *Working Capital Buffer:* €10,000 cash reserve to absorb operating requirements prior to achieving operational break-even.
* **Funding Architecture:** Optimized capital mix comprising €20,000 Owner's Equity (40%) and a €30,000 Commercial Bank Term Loan (60%).

### 2. Operational Economics & Margin Profile
* **Gross Margin Stability:** Sustained at 60.0% through rigorous Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) tracking across local ingredient sourcing and import supply lines.
* **Break-Even Analysis:** Calculated threshold of 84 customers/day at an average order value (AOV) of €10, yielding a monthly break-even revenue requirement of €6,720.
* **Earnings Trajectory:** Revenue expands from €96,000 in Year 1 to €116,688 in Year 5 (5.0% CAGR), expanding net profitability from €7,600 to €13,737 as fixed operational leverage takes effect.

### 3. Balance Sheet Deleveraging & Cash Flow Health
* **Deleveraging Trajectory:** Long-term liabilities steadily amortized from €30,000 down to €22,000 by Year 5.
* **Equity Compounding:** Total retained equity expands from €30,000 in Year 1 to €46,000 by Year 5 through consistent operational cash generation.
* **Cash Flow Discipline:** Positive net operating cash flows generated across all operating periods, ensuring solvency without external equity dilution.

---

## 🛠️ Strategic & Operational Framework
* **Weekend-Only Operational Model:** Structuring operations around peak leisure demand to minimize fixed operating overhead and labor friction.
* **Multicultural Rotating Positioning:** Monthly thematic focus (regional culinary traditions, brewing methodologies, and cultural workshops) validated by consumer survey data indicating 85% concept interest and 70% willingness to pay an experiential premium.
* **Enterprise Risk Management:** Scenario planning addressing operational bottlenecks, seasonal demand volatility, food safety compliance, and supply chain inflation.
